Privacy & Legal Notice — PolicyDelta Radar

PolicyDelta Radar is an automated pattern-matching and text-comparison utility operating in 0€ Validation Mode (no paid checkout, no user accounts, no payment processing).

1. Local-Only Document Comparison

  • All Terms of Service, Privacy Policies, EULAs, and contracts pasted or loaded from disk are processed strictly in local browser memory.
  • No agreement text or diff report is transmitted to Conversionlogic servers, external AI models, or stored in localStorage.
  • No tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or third-party CDNs are loaded.

2. Priority Waitlist Data (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a))

Joining the Custom YAML Rule-Pack & LegalOps Toolkit waitlist is optional and requires checking an un-ticked explicit consent checkbox. Your email address and optional workflow preference are used solely for a one-time notification if PolicyDelta Radar Pro launches. You may withdraw consent and request removal at any time.

3. Informational Utility Scope

PolicyDelta Radar performs automated heuristic keyword, negation, and Unicode sanitization checks across English-language clause patterns. Automated findings are provided purely as an informational review aid and do not substitute for manual human reading and independent verification.
